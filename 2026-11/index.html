Armana · Six-month funding proposal · 50,000 KOIN per month

We are committed to pushing Koinos forward, building new apps that bring new people into the community. We have been doing it since the day mainnet went live: if you have held KOIN in a wallet, looked up a transaction, joined a mining pool or voted on this page, you have likely used our work. We are asking the community to fund the next six months of it.

Who we are. Armana is Julián González and Ron Hamenahem. Ron co-founded Koinos Group and served as its COO until 2025; when the company wound down, he and Julián helped start the Koinos Community Foundation that maintains the chain today. Julián has written Koinos software since the 2020 mining phase: koilib, the JavaScript library most Koinos apps are built on; Kondor, the wallet; 129 commits to the Koinos core repositories; and the Koinos Fund contract that this vote runs on, including the fix that shipped in the October 2025 hardfork. He was the first community developer to ship working products for Koinos: Kondor and koilib were live the day mainnet launched, built before AI coding tools existed.

We have kept shipping since: Fogata (2023), Kondor 1.0 (2024), KoinScan (2025), this KFS voting site (2025), Kondor v2 (2026) and Fogata v2 (2026). KoinScan alone has 41 merged pull requests since February 2025 and 39 changes recorded in its public changelog since January, each linked to commits. Until now, every cost has come out of our own pockets: hosting, domains, the node server and all of the engineering.

Not everything we built stayed up. Armana Portal, launched in 2023, was a visionary social application built on Koinos: a self-custody wallet, a short-video feed, NFT minting and tipping in one mobile app, years ahead of what the network's user base could sustain. It cost more to run than its use justified, so we paused it in 2025 rather than keep spending. Pausing it was a cost decision, not a change of direction. If Portal returns, it will cost a fraction of the old stack, and we will announce it when it ships.

What comes next. Keeping these running is half the work. The other half is new apps for people who have never used Koinos: Kondor mobile first, then more, announced when they ship, not before.

What the funding delivers. Over six months we will:

  1. Keep KoinScan and kfs.koinscan.com online.
  2. Take Fogata v2 out of beta.
  3. Bring an additional full Koinos node online with a public API endpoint, adding capacity and resilience to the network.
  4. Release Kondor mobile as a public beta on the App Store and Google Play.
  5. Publish a changelog entry each month linking that month's work, and a closing report at the end of the term.

We do not promise 24-hour support or user numbers. Anything beyond this list is announced when it ships, and each month's changelog shows what did.

Budget. The request is 50,000 KOIN a month, 300,000 KOIN over six months. It is stated in the unit the fund pays in, not pegged to a dollar figure: if KOIN moves over the term, so does what we receive, the same as for every holder. It is about 37% of what the fund receives each month, so it can be paid alongside Pablo García's proposal without drawing down the treasury. The money covers hosting, a full Koinos node, tooling and the work on the deliverables above. None of it is spent on marketing, token purchases or liquidity.

This is not a salary. At ordinary rates our time would cost more than the fund takes in each month. The request covers what it costs to keep these services online and helps us keep doing the work. We are not billing hours; judge us by what ships, recorded in the monthly changelog.

Disclosure. Armana builds and hosts kfs.koinscan.com, and Julián wrote the fund contract. Votes and payments are enforced by the contract on chain, not by us, and anyone can verify them at 1A5BmMqV5jN5zBrdkhQumAfDZBzXLPBeN9.

Why vote for this. Your vote keeps the tools Koinos depends on running and backs the next apps that bring new people in, from a team that has been here since the first block. We also support Pablo García's proposal, as fellow members of the Koinos Community Foundation, and encourage everyone doing real work for Koinos to apply.
